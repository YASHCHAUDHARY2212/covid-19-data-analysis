🔍 Regional Waves & High-Risk Zones

Analysis reveals catastrophic wave dynamics: India's Delta wave (May 2021) peaked at ~414,000 cases/day, exhausting hospital oxygen reserves. The United States Omicron surge (Jan 2022) set a record with over 1.3 million cases in a single 24-hour period.

📊 Case Fatality Rate (CFR %) Divergence

Rigorous testing and early containment in South Korea (0.11% CFR) and Japan (0.22% CFR) sharply limited mortality. Conversely, nations experiencing healthcare bottlenecks such as Brazil (1.89%) and Peru (>4.0%) sustained high fatality levels.

📈 Mitigating Reporting Artifacts

Raw data exhibited severe weekend reporting dips followed by artificial Monday spikes. Applying a 7-day rolling moving average smoothed operational delays, exposing genuine epidemic curves.
