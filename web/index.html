🔍 Regional Waves & High-Risk Zones

Identified catastrophic regional waves: India's Delta wave (May 2021) peaked at ~414,000 cases/day, severely straining healthcare infrastructure. Meanwhile, the US experienced an Omicron surge (Jan 2022) exceeding 1.3 million cases in a single day.

📊 Case Fatality Rate (CFR) Disparities

Observed significant divergence in healthcare outcomes: high-testing nations like South Korea (0.11%) and Japan (0.22%) maintained minimal fatality ratios, whereas nations facing healthcare saturation (e.g., Brazil at 1.89% and Peru at 4.9%) exhibited severe fatality spikes.

🔬 Smoothing Reporting Anomalies

Weekend reporting lag created artificial cyclical swings in raw data. Implemented a 7-day rolling moving average (MA) algorithm to filter out reporting delays and isolate genuine epidemiological trajectory.
